What is $DOG?
$DOG (DOG•GO•TO•THE•MOON) is the largest community coin on Bitcoin. Technically it's Rune #3, etched inside Bitcoin's own network with the Runes protocol at block 840,000. Supply of 100 billion, open license (CC0), no owner. View on chain.
How was it created? Was it fair?
One of the fairest launches ever. No presale. No team reserve. No discounted investor. $DOG was airdropped free to those who contributed to the network. People active in the Bitcoin and Ordinals ecosystem earned the Runestone, and Runestone holders got the $DOG airdrop (~75,000 wallets). Even the creator took only ~0.008%. Source.
Who controls $DOG today?
No one "owns" $DOG. But we watch concentration closely. We traced a single control cluster that gathered about 12% of supply. It consolidated. It held. The identity is still unknown. Suspicious, documented, not proven. See today's read and the watched wallets.
How do I store $DOG safely?
The most sovereign way is self custody. Take $DOG off the exchange and keep it in your own Bitcoin and Runes wallet that only you control. Pulling coins off the exchange shrinks the float that can be used against the holder. This is education, not financial advice.
Why "don't trust, verify"?
Because everything here is public and reproducible. You don't take our word for it. The data lives on dogdata.xyz, mempool.space and ordinals.com. See the sources and check it yourself.
